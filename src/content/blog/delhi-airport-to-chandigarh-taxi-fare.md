## The Standard Delhi to Chandigarh Taxi Fare

The route from New Delhi to Chandigarh is one of the most frequently traveled highway corridors in North India (via NH44). Because of this high volume, pricing is generally competitive, but hidden costs can often surprise unwary travelers.

With a transparent provider, you should expect the following fixed rates (inclusive of tolls):

- **Sedan (Etios / Dzire):** ₹3,000 – ₹3,500
- **Premium SUV (Innova Crysta):** ₹4,500 – ₹5,000

> **Important Note:** Many offline operators will quote a lower base fare (e.g., ₹2,000) but will force you to pay state entry taxes for Haryana and Punjab, plus multiple toll plazas along the NH44, adding an unexpected ₹1,000+ to your journey. Always ensure your quoted fare is **all-inclusive**.

## Travel Time and Highway Conditions

Under normal traffic conditions, the drive from IGI Airport (Terminal 3 or Terminal 1) to Chandigarh takes approximately **4.5 to 5 hours**. 

The journey primarily utilizes **National Highway 44 (NH44)**, which is an extremely well-maintained, multi-lane expressway. The route bypasses major traffic bottlenecks via the Panipat Elevated Expressway and the Ambala-Chandigarh Expressway.

### Best Places to Stop
If you need a break, the highway is famous for its dhabas. We highly recommend stopping at:
1. **Murthal:** Famous for Amrik Sukhdev Dhaba and Garam Dharam. Perfect for heavy, butter-laden parathas.
2. **Karnal / Kurukshetra:** Modern food courts like Haveli or Oasis offer clean restrooms and diverse food options.
