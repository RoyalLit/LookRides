With the rise of ride-sharing apps, booking a "shared" intercity cab has become a popular budget option for solo travelers. At first glance, splitting the fare seems like a fantastic deal. 

However, for long-distance journeys like Delhi to Chandigarh or Chandigarh to Manali, shared cabs often turn into a nightmare of delays and discomfort. Here is why you should always opt for a private, dedicated taxi.

## 1. Unpredictable Delays and Route Deviations

When you book a shared cab, you are at the mercy of the driver's schedule and the other passengers' locations. 

The driver will often spend the first 1 to 2 hours of your journey driving around congested city traffic just to pick up other passengers. Furthermore, if a co-passenger needs to be dropped off at a remote village off the highway, your 4-hour journey can easily stretch into an exhausting 7-hour ordeal.

## 2. Cramped, Uncomfortable Seating

To maximize profits, shared cab operators often squeeze as many passengers into the vehicle as legally (and sometimes illegally) possible. 

You may find yourself stuck in the middle seat of a small hatchback for 5 hours, with zero elbow room and your luggage crammed onto your lap. This is incredibly dangerous on high-speed highways and guarantees you will arrive at your destination exhausted and sore.

## 3. Security Concerns

When traveling in a shared cab, you are locked in a confined space for hours with complete strangers. While most journeys are fine, incidents of theft, harassment, and altercations between passengers over AC temperature or music volume are unfortunately common.
