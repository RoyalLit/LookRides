Shimla, the Queen of Hills, remains one of the most beloved weekend getaways for travelers from Chandigarh and Delhi. The journey itself—winding through the lower Himalayas—is a significant part of the experience. 

If you are planning to book a **Chandigarh to Shimla taxi**, here is everything you need to know about the route, the fares, and the best places to stop.

## The Route: Himalayan Expressway to NH5

The drive from Chandigarh to Shimla is approximately **115 kilometers** and takes around **3.5 to 4 hours** under normal traffic conditions. The journey begins on the stunning Himalayan Expressway (bypassing the chaotic traffic of Pinjore and Kalka) and merges onto National Highway 5 (NH5).

The four-laning of the Parwanoo-Solan stretch has significantly reduced travel times, but the final ascent from Solan to Shimla still requires an experienced hill driver due to sharp hairpin bends and steep inclines.

## Must-Visit Stops Along the Way

1. **Timber Trail, Parwanoo:** Famous for its cable car ride offering panoramic views of the Shivalik range. A great spot for a quick coffee.
2. **Dharampur:** Known for the legendary Giani Da Dhaba. It’s the perfect place to grab a bite before continuing the climb.
3. **Barog:** A quaint stop known for its historic railway station and beautiful pine forests.
