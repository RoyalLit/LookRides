Manali is one of Himachal Pradesh's most spectacular and highly-visited hill stations. Nestled at the northern end of the Kullu Valley, its appeal changes dramatically with the seasons. 

Whether you're hunting for pristine white snow, lush green trekking trails, or a cool summer escape from the North Indian heat, choosing the right time to visit is crucial. Here is LookRides' complete guide to the best time to visit Manali.

## 1. Winter (October to February) - For Snow Lovers

If you want to see Manali blanketed in snow, winter is the only time to go. 

- **Temperature:** Drops below freezing (-5°C to 10°C).
- **Highlights:** Snowfall usually begins in late December. This is the peak time for honeymooners and winter sports enthusiasts. Rohtang Pass is usually closed during this time due to heavy snow, but Solang Valley becomes a hub for skiing, snowboarding, and snowmobiling.
- **Travel Tip:** The road from Chandigarh to Manali can be treacherous during active snowfall. Always book an experienced local hill-station driver (like the chauffeurs at LookRides) who understand how to navigate black ice and steep icy inclines safely.

## 2. Summer (March to June) - The Peak Tourist Season

Summer is the most popular time to visit Manali, offering a perfect escape from the scorching plains of Punjab and Delhi.

- **Temperature:** Pleasant and cool (10°C to 25°C).
- **Highlights:** The snow melts, revealing vibrant green valleys, blooming apple orchards, and clear blue skies. This is the ideal time for adventure sports like paragliding in Solang Valley, river rafting in the Beas River (Kullu), and trekking in the surrounding Himalayan ranges. Rohtang Pass opens in late May/early June.
- **Travel Tip:** Because this is peak season, highways can experience heavy traffic, especially on weekends. Booking your [Chandigarh to Manali taxi](/routes/chandigarh-to-manali) in advance is highly recommended to secure the best rates.

## 3. Monsoon (July to September) - The Off-Season

The monsoon season transforms Manali into an impossibly lush, green paradise, but it comes with significant travel risks.

- **Temperature:** Humid and cool (15°C to 20°C).
- **Highlights:** The Beas River swells, the waterfalls are at their most powerful, and the valley is remarkably quiet and uncrowded. Hotel rates are at their lowest.
- **Travel Tip:** We generally advise **caution** when traveling during the monsoon. The Chandigarh-Manali highway is prone to sudden landslides and flash floods during heavy downpours. If you must travel, keep your itinerary flexible and rely on real-time weather updates.
